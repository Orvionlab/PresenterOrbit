Presenter Orbit sample content

These themes, stage layouts, and example texts were authored for Presenter Orbit.
They are distributed under GPL-3.0-only. See the project's LICENSE.

Black, White, Harbor, and Afterglow provide general text, title and subtitle,
and lower-third layouts. The stage layouts demonstrate live text, previews,
speaker notes, stage messages, countdowns, and the system clock.

The samples use built-in shapes and system fonts. The app creates an editable
copy in each new workspace.
